Strategy & Pitfalls

First scout, then act,
manage Resistance.

1
Gather Intelligence — Prioritize interviews and social gatherings to unlock preferences and hidden relationships.
2
Target Bridge Nodes — Converting high-influence stakeholders automatically spreads change to more people.
3
Control Institutional Resistance — Institutional actions are fast but costly; relieve pressure before it hits the cap.
  • ⚠️
    Don't rely on a single action type — A one-dimensional strategy will hit a ceiling; combine approaches.
  • ⚠️
    Some stakeholders require more resources — Certain key figures have higher conversion thresholds; use interviews to uncover their true stance.
  • 🔍
    Some stakeholders' true stance must be discovered through interviews — Their real position won't be shown until you interview them or their associates.
